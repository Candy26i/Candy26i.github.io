Jingxuan Li 李婧瑄

I'm a second-year PhD student in Statistics and Data Science at Northwestern University. I received my B.S. in Mathematics of Computation from the University of California, Los Angeles, in 2025.

I work on LLM agents and vision-language model evaluation with Professor Ying Nian Wu at UCLA. My current project is about delegation in multi-agent systems: when asking another model for help improves an answer, and when it makes things worse.

Research experience

MARGENT

May 2025–present · Advisor: Ying Nian Wu, UCLA

I build paired reasoning branches to compare an extra agent call with committing to the current answer. I use these comparisons to train a manager that routes between extractor, reasoner, and verifier agents, and evaluate it on MedQA, MMLU-Pro, GPQA, and AQuA-RAT.

Value-Spectrum

September 2023–May 2025 · Advisor: Ying Nian Wu, UCLA

I built pipelines to collect social media imagery and model responses, and used Schwartz's human value dimensions to compare VLM preferences. I also developed prompts for assigning personas to models and tested how consistently different VLMs followed those personas.
